The experience, step by step

What to expect when we work together.

A straightforward process, clear expectations, and complete freedom to decide what happens next.

01 — INTRODUCTION

Start with a conversation.

FROM THE BUSINESS OWNER

Tell me about the company, your goals, what's working, and where you'd like a fresh perspective. No documents are needed for the first conversation.

FROM WILL WEBB

I'll listen, ask questions, share initial thinking, and determine whether there's a strong mutual fit.

02 — GETTING STARTED

Agree on the approach.

FROM THE BUSINESS OWNER

If we both want to proceed, agree on priorities, identify a main contact, and share relevant information you're comfortable providing.

FROM WILL WEBB

I'll confirm the scope and confidentiality expectations of the complimentary 30-day review, and request only information relevant to the agreed priorities.

03 — INDEPENDENT REVIEW

I get to work.

FROM THE BUSINESS OWNER

Respond to occasional clarifying questions or connect me with the right team member if useful. No need to manage the analysis.

FROM WILL WEBB

I'll review the business, connect the dots, identify potential opportunities, and develop practical recommendations supported by available information.

04 — OPPORTUNITY CALL

Review the possibilities.

FROM THE BUSINESS OWNER

Discuss the findings, challenge assumptions, ask questions, and decide which opportunities to pursue, defer, or leave alone.

FROM WILL WEBB

I'll explain what I found, why it matters, what I'd prioritize, and the choices involved. Recommendations are yours to use.

05 — NEXT STEPS

You choose the direction.

FROM THE BUSINESS OWNER

Take the recommendations and proceed independently, take time to decide, or explore having me lead the execution.

FROM WILL WEBB

If decisions are pending, I'll continue the agreed review within the 30-day window. If you'd like execution, we'll define a separate paid engagement first.

Simple to get started

What will I need from you?

We start with your perspective, not a lengthy questionnaire or a request for access to everything.

To begin

Only the essentials:

  • A conversation about your business and goals
  • What you'd like to improve or explore
  • One point of contact
  • Agreement on the initial review priorities

If useful during the review

Depending on the opportunities we're investigating:

  • A business overview or relevant process notes
  • Sales, marketing or operational performance summaries
  • Existing systems and workflows, described at a high level
  • Introductions to key team members

Full financial statements, administrative access, and sensitive company records are not required by default. Information requests are agreed in advance and handled confidentially.

Where I contribute

The business as a whole—not just one department.

What we examine depends on your business. I focus on the opportunities that matter, not a fixed checklist of services.

  • Growth strategy and market opportunity
  • Sales and customer conversion
  • Marketing and positioning
  • Customer experience and retention
  • Operations and execution
  • Teams, roles and decision-making
  • Technology, systems and practical AI
  • Measurement and priorities

The complimentary 30-day engagement covers agreed analysis and recommendations—not a financial audit or unlimited implementation. Execution begins only under a separate paid agreement.

After we discuss opportunities

What happens after the opportunity call?

You stay in control. Identifying an opportunity doesn't mean you have to make a decision on the spot.

Not ready to decide?

No pressure. Within our agreed complimentary 30-day period, I'll continue relevant analysis, refine options and document next steps. At the end, the findings are yours to use as you choose.

Ready to take action?

If you'd like me to lead implementation, we'll agree on the work, authority, responsibilities and compensation before execution starts. That is a separate paid relationship, not an automatic continuation.

You keep the recommendations—either way.

My goal is to provide insight, clarity and a practical direction. You can move forward independently, with your existing team, or with me in a separately negotiated execution role.

The introductory review is complimentary. Implementation is paid and never begins without a new agreement.
